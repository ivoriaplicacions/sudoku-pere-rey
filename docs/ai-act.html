English

Maestros del Sudoku is not an AI system within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act). Recital 12 excludes traditional software that only follows rules defined by humans to execute operations automatically.

Sudoku puzzles are generated offline with a deterministic backtracking solver that checks for a unique solution. The app does not train models, does not infer from user behaviour, does not generate open-ended content, and does not adapt after deployment. The player makes every move.

Therefore:

  • It is not prohibited AI (Title II).
  • It is not high-risk AI (Annex III): no biometrics, no education scoring, no employment, no essential services.
  • It is not a general-purpose AI model (GPAI).
  • Article 50 transparency duties for AI that interacts with humans do not apply: there is no chatbot or synthetic media.

If a future version adds machine learning or generative AI, Ivori Aplicacions will classify it before release, update this statement and the governance record, and apply the matching AI Act obligations.

Català

Maestros del Sudoku no és un sistema d’IA als efectes del Reglament (UE) 2024/1689. El considerant 12 n’exclou el programari tradicional que només executa regles definides per persones.

Els sudokus es generen fora de línia amb un solucionador determinista (backtracking) que verifica solució única. No hi ha entrenament de models, ni inferència sobre el jugador, ni contingut generatiu, ni adaptació després del desplegament. El jugador fa totes les jugades.
