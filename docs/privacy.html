English

Progress, settings and restored purchases are stored on this device only. The app does not create user accounts, does not use advertising identifiers and does not sell personal data.

In-app purchases (content packs at 0,99 € each; Pack 1 is free) are processed by Google Play or the App Store. Those stores handle payment data under their own policies. You can restore non-consumable purchases from the in-app store.
