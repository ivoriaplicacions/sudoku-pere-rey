English

This record is the governance file for the consumer app Maestros del Sudoku (bundle com.ivoriaplicacions.maestrosdelsudoku, version 1.0.0).

  • Provider / publisher: Ivori Aplicacions
  • Purpose: offline Sudoku puzzles sold as one-time content packs (0,99 € / pack; Pack 1 free)
  • AI inventory: no AI systems, no GPAI, no training datasets
  • Human oversight: all board decisions are made by the player
  • Logging: progress stays on device; no remote telemetry
  • Personal data: see privacy policy (GDPR local processing)
  • Change control: any feature that infers, recommends or generates content with ML must be classified under the AI Act before shipping
  • Review: this file is reviewed when the app version or legal URLs change
  • Complaints / support: GitHub issues
