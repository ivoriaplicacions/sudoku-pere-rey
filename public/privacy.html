English

Progress, settings and restored purchases are stored on this device only (GDPR: local processing, no user account). The app does not create accounts, does not use advertising identifiers, does not sell personal data and does not send gameplay data to our servers.

What we receive: nothing. The publisher operates no server and receives no personal data from the app, so there is no processing on our side to base on a legal ground, no retention period on our side, and no international transfers made by us. Data stays on your device until you delete it.

In-app purchases (content packs at 0,99 € each; Pack 1 is free) are processed by Google Play or the App Store. Those stores are the sellers of record and act as independent controllers for payment and account data under their own privacy policies; we only receive an anonymous confirmation that a product identifier has been purchased. You can restore non-consumable purchases from the in-app store.

Your rights

You may exercise the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability (arts. 15–22 GDPR) by writing to hola@ivoriaplicacions.es. Because we hold no personal data about you, we will normally answer that there is nothing to access or erase, and point you to the local deletion steps below. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es).

To erase local data: uninstall the app or clear app storage.

This product is not an AI system under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. See the AI Act statement and governance.
